# Research, helpers and great innovators

## Playable in 0.3.0

Research is a quota resource distinct from VP. Free discovery grants three initial credits, one per five genuinely new collection discoveries, capped at five. Sprints grant two each round with no rollover. Do not refill quota by replaying a recipe or double-clicking a completed round.

A research focus follows prerequisite recipes toward a target innovation, observing the active era and world condition. The output must be undiscovered, and both ingredients must already be available. Revealing a recipe costs one credit; failure or re-reading a current result is free. Research never grants the resulting item or VP automatically.

The Camp Tinkerer is explicitly fictional/unnamed. Stone Age named historical scientists would be misleading, so none are assigned. It needs Firecraft + Toolmaking and one recruitment credit. One team slot is available. The player enables automation explicitly; each successful automated craft costs one credit. All ingredients are reusable in this concept prototype, so resource availability means the item has been discovered in the active mode and is allowed by the era. A consumable inventory requires a future engine transaction, not a frontend check.

Automatic work happens at most once every 12 seconds on a visible, active free-discovery page. It pauses while the player uses either slot; it does not overwrite the workbench, act in a sprint, consume credits on failure, craft future-era items, farm repeats, or catch up when returning from a hidden tab.

## Future mode: Innovators

Proposed data contract (not active content):

- scientist ID, localized name, historically checked era, specialty and reference source;
- unlock conditions based on knowledge, era, first inventions or events;
- recruitment policy: either a limited team quota or a separate research budget, avoiding expenditure of final VP by default;
- limited team slots, research speed, eligible recipe tags, explicit queued jobs;
- reservation of ingredient quantities and server-authoritative completion for multiplayer;
- research result states: locked, researchable, revealed, craftable, discovered;
- once-only invention milestone rewards with stable IDs and a versioned reward ledger.

A flight-era pack could recognize the **Wright brothers** when a clearly defined powered-flight milestone is reached. This is a proposed game unlock, not a claim that inventing a plane creates a historical person. Aircraft recipes, the flight era, specific historical biographies, recruitment balance, or their art are not implemented. Verify names, dates and contributions from primary sources before publishing a historical content pack.
